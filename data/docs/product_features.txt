Northwind Analytics — Product Features

Dashboards: Pre-built views for revenue trend, new vs. returning customers,
and average order value, refreshed daily from connected store data.

Churn Prediction: A machine learning model scores each customer on a 0-100
churn risk scale based on recency of purchase, order frequency, and support
ticket volume. Available on Growth and Enterprise tiers.

Cohort Analysis: Groups customers by signup month and tracks retention across
subsequent months, so a team can see whether a specific onboarding campaign
improved long-term retention.

Integrations: Native connectors for Shopify, WooCommerce, and BigCommerce.
Enterprise customers can request a custom API integration for platforms not
natively supported.

Alerts: Configurable email or Slack alerts when a customer's churn risk score
crosses a threshold, or when weekly revenue drops more than 15% versus the
prior week.

Data Retention: Raw event data is retained for 24 months. Aggregated reports
are retained indefinitely unless the account is deleted.
