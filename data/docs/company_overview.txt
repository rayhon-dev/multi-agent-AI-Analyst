Northwind Analytics — Company Overview

Northwind Analytics is a B2B SaaS company founded in 2019 that helps mid-sized
e-commerce businesses understand their customer data. The core product is a
dashboard that connects to a merchant's order history and produces reports on
revenue, customer retention, and churn risk.

The company has three pricing tiers: Starter ($49/month), Growth ($199/month),
and Enterprise (custom pricing). Starter includes basic dashboards. Growth
adds cohort analysis and churn prediction. Enterprise adds a dedicated account
manager, custom integrations, and a service-level agreement (SLA) of 99.9%
uptime.

Northwind's mission is to make customer analytics accessible to businesses
that cannot afford a full in-house data team. As of the most recent fiscal
year, the company serves just over 1,200 paying customers, primarily in North
America and Western Europe.

The company is headquartered in Austin, Texas, with a remote engineering team
distributed across four time zones.
