Northwind Analytics — Customer Support FAQ

Q: What is your refund policy?
A: Customers on monthly plans can cancel at any time and are not billed for
the following month. We do not offer partial refunds for the current billing
period. Annual plans can be refunded on a pro-rated basis within the first 60
days.

Q: Why do customers most commonly cancel their subscription?
A: Based on exit surveys collected by the support team, the most common
reasons customers churn are: (1) the dashboard did not integrate with their
e-commerce platform, (2) the price was too high relative to their order
volume, (3) they hired an in-house analyst and no longer needed the tool, and
(4) low usage — teams that log in less than once a month are the most likely
to cancel within a quarter.

Q: How do I export my data before canceling?
A: Go to Settings > Data Export. Exports are available in CSV format and
include the last 24 months of processed data. Exports are not available after
an account has been fully deleted.

Q: Is there a free trial?
A: Yes, all new signups get a 14-day free trial of the Growth tier with no
credit card required.
